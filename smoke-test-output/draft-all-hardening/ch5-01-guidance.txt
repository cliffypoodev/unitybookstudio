CHAPTER 5: The Old Mill

Margot drives to the old mill before dawn, carrying both ledgers. The mill is partially collapsed but the meeting room Maude described is intact. Margot finds names carved into a support beam — the same three families from the receipt. She photographs the evidence. As she turns to leave, headlights sweep across the mill's windows.
