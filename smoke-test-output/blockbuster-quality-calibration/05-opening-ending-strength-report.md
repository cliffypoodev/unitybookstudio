## New Module Impact

`SIGNATURE_VOICE_BLOCK` now explicitly bans:
- Lesson-statement endings ("And that was when she realized...")
- Thesis statement endings ("The truth was...")
- Planning statement endings ("the next phase")
- Requires endings on **image, action, or unresolved tension**
